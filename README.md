## Install

1. Download **Akshar-Setup.exe** from the [latest release](https://github.com/Asif-shaik269/akshar-releases/releases/latest).
2. Open the file and follow the installer.
3. Sign in with your school account. Your records sync through the Principal's Google Drive.

> **Seeing "Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
> This can appear for new software. Only run installers downloaded from this page.

## Updating

Download the latest installer and run it over your current version. Your school data stays in place.

## Your data

Akshar works fully offline. Your school's records live on your own devices and in a folder in the
Principal's Google Drive. Teclusion AI does not store or see them.
See our [Privacy Policy](https://teclusion.ai/akshar/privacy.html) and [Security](https://teclusion.ai/akshar/security.html) pages.
